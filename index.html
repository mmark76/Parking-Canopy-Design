PARKING CANOPY DESIGN

Project overview

Four working areas, each linked to its authoritative project documentation.

01

SITE & PARKING LAYOUT

Existing operation retained

The project covers parcels 190 and 491. The existing parking layout is retained; parcel 491 is treated as two independent sub-parking areas, and access and manoeuvring must remain functional.

Open site data →

02

CANOPY DESIGN

Rectilinear Γ and Τ systems

Confirmed requirements use Γ canopies for single rows and Τ canopies for double rows, with galvanized structural steel, perforated shade fabric, 2.40 m minimum clear height, no photovoltaics and no gutters.

Working assumptions: concept geometry and module dimensions remain preliminary and require design development.

Requirements Design basis

03

STRUCTURAL DESIGN

Basis identified; sizing pending

Design is based on applicable Eurocodes and Cyprus National Annexes. Wind is considered a critical action; structural sizing and foundation design remain pending. Preliminary assumptions are not final design.

Open design basis →

04

TENDER & PROCUREMENT

Performance-led public tender

The procurement approach uses performance-based specifications, supplier and brand neutrality, and technically equivalent solutions. The BOQ and tender package are future project outputs.

Open procurement strategy →
